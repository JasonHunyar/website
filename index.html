I am an independent investigative journalist and the writer of FOIA Around And Find Out. My work centers on open records requests, and I report on the surveillance technology companies and the government agencies that purchase their products. I am also a co-founder of Dunwoody for Privacy, a nonpartisan community transparency organization.

Who I'd like to meet: Editors, fellow reporters, researchers, and anyone with information the public deserves to see.

Currently digging into: Surveillance vendors and lobbyists connected to them. In general: open records, lawsuits, hidden web pages, and careful review of government contracts.
